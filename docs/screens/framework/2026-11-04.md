## Verdict

**Drop**, at Gate 3, by the architect's ruling of 2026-11-04: framework drops Gemma 4 26B-A4B QAT
serving agent and vision, since b11960's Vulkan backend can't serve the 15-slot section, so the
trade of more sessions at full context can't hold there. The keep rule
([SKILL.md](../../../.claude/skills/hosting-upgrades/SKILL.md), "The screen mode", step 8),
condition by condition:

1. **Gates:** fail, at Gate 3 (engine). At 16 slots the load aborted in auto-fit (cf. #29082); at
   15 the second request aborted the instance (#29221), whose fix #25584 is unmerged. The parser
   clause: #29655 doesn't count (the architect's ruling, 2026-11-04).
2. **Capability:** not measured on framework; BFCL didn't run.
3. **Tool reliability:** not measured on framework; conform didn't run.
4. **Completeness:** fails. Shape A 1/8, shape B 0/32, long A 0/4, long B 0/16; the sweep, vision
   A and B, BFCL, and conform didn't run.
5. **Speed:** not measured on framework. Shape A's one request read 37.8 tok/s, which places no
   cell; long A and long B completed none. Set A's same-session figures, for the record: long A
   prefill 932 tok/s (derived) with TTFT median 8.8 s, long B ITL median 61.4 ms.
6. **Capacity:** not measured on framework. The router reported 15 slots of 131,072 tokens, but the
   sweep didn't run. Set A's highest level at ITL median ≤100 ms: 4 streams.
7. **Memory:** not measured on framework: the 10.99 GiB after phase 2's long B is E4B's and
   granite's alone. Set A read 92.22 GiB.
8. **Vision:** not measured on the agent section. On the vision section, every request was
   answered within 10 s (at most 8.00 s), with 8 of 8 and 32 of 32 replies reasoning in the
   channel and 0 in the text.

The rule gives a drop, and the architect's ruling is the same; there is no overrule. Rejected by
the architect: a framework-only 3-slot section, which fails capacity by construction.

**The record of the incumbent**, set A on framework at b11960, phase 1: anchor A 43.79 and B 75.94
tok/s (TTFT 703 and 2,300 ms over DERP, ITL 19.70 and 45.35 ms); long A 26.07 tok/s, TTFT 8.8 s,

whole set loaded; the sweep's highest level at ITL ≤100 ms, 4 streams; the vision floor held, at
