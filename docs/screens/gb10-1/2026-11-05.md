## Verdict

gpt-oss-120b unloaded, fails BFCL multi-turn's floor on gb10-1 at b12098. Every other condition
holds. The keep rule ([SKILL.md](../../../.claude/skills/hosting-upgrades/SKILL.md), "The screen
mode", step 8), condition by condition, with gpt-oss-120b's same-session figures from phase 1
beside Gemma's:

1. **Gates:** hold, but for Gate 4, which is the capability condition below. Origin, integrity
   (the template identical), the floor, fit, and the engine at b12098 pass ("Gates"). The parser
   clause: no open tracker report has llama.cpp's parser rejecting Gemma 4's tool calls; #29655
   doesn't count (the architect's ruling, 2026-11-04).
2. **Capability:** **fails** on multi-turn. BFCL single-turn 0.780 (78 of 100) against 0.630:
   holds. BFCL multi-turn 5 passing of 19 done at the 45-minute cap, at most 6 / 20 = 0.30 on its

   its own runs); its first 4 multi-turn samples passed 0 of 4 in the nocache check, a partial list
   that is no figure.

   dropped-image n/a; fit yes.

   count, phase 1's long A on its retry (the retry decision, 2026-11-05), and the sweep's
   incomplete requests are its time limit, by design. BFCL multi-turn stopped at 19 of 20 at its

### Findings

- **The #31620 break.** llama.cpp b12100 and later (#31620, #31639) match an -hf tag only
  after `-`, `.` or `/`, and Google's QAT GGUFs for 26B-A4B, 31B, and E4B put `_` before it,
  so none of their sections resolves on those builds: on this host the vision model and Gemma's
  agent section among them. gb10-1 is held at b12098, the newest nightly before the change,
  until Google renames its files or llama.cpp accepts `_` (the descriptor's watch item). The
  break is also a comment on the gb10 pin in spike-model-engines' `host/llama.env` (986e738),
  through the screen branch's pull request (the architect, 2026-11-05).
- **The first anchors moved +5.0%** between the 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-05 readings on the same
  build and kernel, across the 2026-11-04 16:02 reboot ("Cells", the engine move).
- **The prompt cache on Gemma's 16-slot section.** On b12098 the maintainers' cache design still
  stalls multi-turn decode (3,271 idle-slot saves; long replies at 8-9 tok/s), though b12098 carries
  #31391; with no prompt cache, nothing evicts a slot's 32 context checkpoints of 200 MiB, and free
  memory fell to 0.69 GiB. Two checkpoints a slot removed both: no saves, long replies at 21.3
  tok/s, memory steady at 38.4 GiB ("Cells", the configuration).
- **BFCL sends no `max_tokens`**, so a looping reply runs until the cap: 23,306 tokens in one
  multi-turn sample on the final section, 9,027 in one on the nocache arm.
- **The sweep's output figure** counts only the requests completed in each 60 s level, which made
  the earlier records' "flat 68 tok/s" ("Cells", the sweep's measure).
